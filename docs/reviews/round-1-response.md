# Review round 1 — voice and fidelity

Reviewer brief: compare the rewritten CV with Natalie's source CV line by line,
flag voice drift, invented claims and lost substance.

## What the review found

- The profile was already close to her own words, but the final sentence
  introduced "portfolio" and described the PMQ awkwardly.
- Six achievement fragments contained connective detail that was not in the
  source ("moving from fragmented local practice", "coordinating partners,
  reporting and delivery milestones", "side by side", "end to end",
  "Defining requirements, evaluating suppliers…").
- The LMS/CRM procurement appeared three times; "circa 100 volunteers" twice;
  "many concurrent projects" twice.
- *Areas of expertise* had replaced her vocabulary — strategy design,
  communication, public speaking, negotiation, copy editing, translation,
  teaching — with delivery-process nouns. The reviewer called this the single
  largest voice loss.
- The business analysis and user research workshops achievement had been lost.
- The member outcome at Crisis Skylight ("personal and social skills, work
  experience and positive social networks") had been lost.
- The reviewer queried the APM qualification date and the SCQF detail.

## What was changed

- Restored her vocabulary to *Areas of expertise*, including strategy design,
  negotiation and influencing, public speaking and presentation, copy editing
  and writing, translation, and both languages.
- Restored the business analysis and user research workshops bullet.
- Restored the member outcome at Crisis Skylight.
- Restored the NVQ 100% pass rate and the CLIL detail in the teaching role.
- Removed the invented connective clauses and the duplicated bullets.
- Restored "exceptional" and the full stop before "Adaptable".
- Restored "leading the implementation of" the benefits framework.
- Changed the header title from "Programme & Project Manager" to a role
  descriptor, and used "and" in prose.
- Kept her personal note off the PDF and on the website, where it does not
  compete with evidence.

## Where the review was overruled

**The APM qualification.** The reviewer read the source CV, which says
"September 2026: currently working towards APM PMQ". The certificate supplied
separately is dated **07/10/2026** and states the qualification was **awarded**,
at **SCQF Level 7, 6 credit points**. The certificate is newer and authoritative,
so the CV keeps "awarded October 2026" and the SCQF detail. This is recorded in
`docs/design/fact-sources.md` so the claim can be defended at interview.

**"Portfolio".** The reviewer treated it as scope inflation. Natalie leads three
named concurrent programmes; "portfolio" is the accurate collective noun and the
word that carries a programme manager into portfolio-level conversations. It is
used once in the profile and once as a section heading, not scattered.

**Hobbies.** The reviewer suggested restoring a personal line. It is on the
website, not the PDF: at two pages, every line has to earn its place, and senior
recruitment guidance is consistent that hobbies are filler on a CV.

## Remaining judgement calls for Natalie

- The Guide Dogs service budget is unknown; the CV says "business planned
  service delivery against budget" without inventing a figure.
- No line management is claimed at Barnardo's because the source does not
  evidence any. The CV shows matrix and partner leadership instead.
- No realised-benefit percentage is claimed because the source has none.
