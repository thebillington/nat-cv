# Review round 2 — market and seniority

Reviewer: a UK senior programme-management hiring panel. Verdict: 6.5/10,
"strong raw material, positioned one level below its own content".

## What the review found

- The first ten seconds were spent on soft adjectives, while three named
  national programmes, a £2.5m DfE programme and a 900-property operating model
  sat in the third sentence.
- "Selected outcomes" contained three "Leading…" statements, so it promised
  outcomes and delivered activity — and duplicated the Barnardo's bullets.
- No total portfolio value, no headcount, no realised benefit with a number.
- Missing senior vocabulary that she can truthfully claim: portfolio governance,
  assurance, dependency management, transition to business-as-usual.
- Public-sector framework vocabulary (Green Book, Five Case, gate reviews,
  GMPP) was correctly absent and should stay absent.
- "Programme & Project Manager" hedged; the market rate for that title is
  ~£46.6k, against ~£64.4k for Senior Programme Manager.
- Trusteeship was buried below a 2011 copy-editing course.

## What was changed

- Profile reorganised so the scope lands in the second sentence: national
  transformation portfolio, £2.5m DfE programme, c.900 properties.
- Title changed to "Programme Manager — Transformation, Governance and
  Benefits", which is both true and the £64k conversation.
- "Selected outcomes" became **Current portfolio**, with three programme-scope
  statements rather than three "Leading…" claims.
- Barnardo's bullets now lead with mechanism and governance rather than
  repeating the portfolio block.
- Added "portfolio governance", "assurance and dependency management" and
  "business readiness" to the expertise block.
- Moved **Board and voluntary roles** above Qualifications, with the trustee
  role first.
- Removed the duplicated "many concurrent projects" bullet and the third
  mention of the LMS/CRM.
- Kept "a £2.5 million Department for Education-funded programme" rather than
  claiming budget authority that the source does not evidence.

## Where the review was not followed

**The suggested profile rewrite** was a strong senior profile but almost none of
it was in Natalie's voice, and it added "public-sector partnerships" and
"trustee of a Nottinghamshire charity" as framing. The scope was moved forward;
the sentences stayed hers.

**A total portfolio value** was not added. Only the £2.5m component is
evidenced; a total would have to be invented. If Natalie knows the combined
value, it should be added to `data/profile.yml` and will flow through.

**Headcount** was not added for Barnardo's, because the source evidences no
direct reports there. Adding a plausible number would fail the first
interview question.
## Open questions for Natalie

These are the panel's likely first questions and cannot be answered from the
source documents:

1. What is the combined value of the programmes you currently lead?
2. Do you have direct reports or a matrix team at Barnardo's, and how many?
3. Is the £2.5m DfE budget yours to manage, or funding held elsewhere?
4. Is there one benefit you have realised with a number attached?
5. Is there a programme that went amber/red that you recovered or re-baselined?
6. Did you contribute to a business case for New Horizons or Property
   Enhancement?
7. Is there a LinkedIn URL to add to the contact line?

Any answer that can be evidenced should be added to `data/`; nothing here should
be guessed.
